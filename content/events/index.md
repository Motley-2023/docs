## Events & Activities

The MIBT Cardano Hub – Zambia organises regular events to support learning, collaboration, and innovation within the Cardano, Web3, AI, and emerging technology ecosystem.

## Types of Events

- Community meetups and awareness sessions  
- Technical workshops and bootcamps  
- Hackathons and innovation challenges  
- Partner-led and campus-based engagements
- Monthly webinars and Summits
- Conferences on emerging technology  

## Purpose of Events

Events serve as entry points for new participants, opportunities for skill-building, and spaces for collaboration between developers, students, educators, and MSMEs.

All events are documented to support shared learning and ecosystem visibility.
