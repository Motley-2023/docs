## High-Level Overview

## Hub Context & Narrative

The **MIBT Cardano Hub – Zambia** is anchored in Kitwe and serves the Copperbelt, Zambia, and the wider AfCFTA ecosystem. The Hub exists to support **community-led innovation** in Cardano, Web3, AI, blockchain, and emerging technologies.

Our work focuses on **empowering youth, women, educators, professionals, and MSMEs** to participate meaningfully in decentralized and digital economies.

---

## Why the Hub Exists

Many communities across Zambia face limited access to emerging technologies, technical skills, and innovation networks. The Hub responds by creating **open learning environments**, shared documentation, and collaborative programs that reduce barriers to entry and support real-world experimentation.

Rather than importing solutions, the Hub prioritises **local context**, community participation, and practical problem-solving.

---

## How We Organise

The Hub organises through:
- community meetups and learning sessions  
- workshops, bootcamps, and hackathons  
- documentation-first knowledge sharing  
- collaboration with local, national, and regional partners  

This approach ensures that innovation is **inclusive, practical, and grounded in real needs**.

---

## Key Focus Areas

- **Cardano & Web3 education**
- **AI and emerging technology awareness**
- **Startup and MSME innovation support**
- **Open documentation and knowledge sharing**
- **Regional ecosystem collaboration**
