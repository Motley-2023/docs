# Core Processes
## Hub Processes

The Hub operates through simple, transparent processes that support coordination, participation, and continuity.

## Key Processes

- Event planning and coordination  
- Community onboarding and engagement  
- Documentation and publishing  
- Project support and incubation  

These processes help maintain clarity, accountability, and shared understanding across the community.
