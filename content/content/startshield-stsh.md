## Platform Overview

StartShield-STSH is a decentralized insurance and risk management platform designed to support underserved sectors across Africa.

The platform provides protection and financial infrastructure for:

• smallholder farmers  
• MSMEs and startups  
• cooperatives  
• small-scale miners  
• rural communities

## STSH Token Ecosystem

Total supply: **2,000,000,000 STSH**

### STSH-U
Utility token used for platform transactions and governance.

### STSH-R
Real World Asset token representing assets including:

• seeds  
• agrochemicals  
• livestock  
• chicks  
• fingerlings  
• mining commodities  
• real estate

### STSH-X
Insurance and risk coverage token supporting decentralized protection mechanisms.
