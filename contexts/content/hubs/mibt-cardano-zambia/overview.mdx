# Overview

The **MIBT Cardano Hub – Zambia** is a community-driven Web3 innovation and developer hub operating from **Kitwe and Lusaka, Zambia**, under the **MIBT Global Ecosystem**.

The Hub empowers **youth, women, educators, professionals, developers, entrepreneurs, researchers, and MSMEs** across Zambia and the wider African and AfCFTA ecosystem to learn, build, collaborate, and create practical solutions using **Cardano, blockchain, Web3, AI, and emerging technologies**.

Our focus is not technology for its own sake. We support the development of **real-world, scalable solutions** that strengthen financial inclusion, digital enterprise, trade, insurance, education, agriculture, mining, and community development.

## About the Hub

The MIBT Cardano Hub – Zambia forms part of the broader **MIBT Global Ecosystem** and contributes to Africa's participation in the Cardano innovation journey.

The Hub brings together:

- Developers and builders
- Entrepreneurs and MSMEs
- Students, educators, and researchers
- Women and youth innovators
- Web3 communities and ecosystem partners
- Institutions working on digital transformation and financial inclusion

Through training, bootcamps, hackathons, research, incubation, ecosystem collaboration, and project development, the Hub helps move ideas from **learning to building to real-world adoption**.

## Locations

### Kitwe

Kitwe serves as a key **developer, innovation, and technology hub**, with activities including:

- Developer training and Web3 education
- Hackathons and innovation labs
- Cardano community activities
- Mining technology and resource-transparency research
- Startup and prototype development

### Lusaka

Lusaka supports **ecosystem coordination, enterprise innovation, partnerships, and incubation**, including:

- Startup and MSME development
- Digital enterprise and trade innovation
- Institutional and ecosystem engagement
- Research collaboration
- National and continental coordination

Together, Kitwe and Lusaka provide a distributed innovation network connecting local builders with the broader **Zambian, Southern African, and African Web3 ecosystem**.

## Core Innovations

The Hub supports a growing portfolio of African-focused innovations, including:

- **StartShield-STSH** – Web3 InsureTech and real-world asset infrastructure built on Cardano
- **UhuruLang** – an African-first programming language initiative designed to make programming and Web3 development more accessible through African languages
- **ASME-X Mining** – mining intelligence, traceability, and resource-transparency infrastructure
- **VillageChain Bank** – community-focused decentralized financial infrastructure
- **STSH-Trade** – digital enterprise identity and AfCFTA trade facilitation infrastructure

## Innovation & Community Journey

The Hub uses a practical journey model:

**Learn → Build → Test → Collaborate → Launch → Scale**

Participants are encouraged to move beyond awareness and develop solutions that can create measurable economic and social value.
