# Overview

The **MIBT Cardano Hub – Zambia** is a Web3 innovation and developer hub operating in **Kitwe and Lusaka**, under the **MIBT Global Ecosystem**. The hub focuses on building real-world blockchain solutions on the **Cardano network** while empowering developers, entrepreneurs, researchers, and innovators across Zambia and the broader African region.

Through developer training, hackathons, research collaboration, and startup incubation, the hub supports the creation of scalable Web3 infrastructure addressing real-world challenges in areas such as financial inclusion, digital trade, mining innovation, education, and entrepreneurship.

---

# Locations

The hub operates across two key innovation locations:

**Kitwe**
- Developer training
- Hackathons and innovation labs
- Mining technology research (ASME-X)

**Lusaka**
- Ecosystem coordination
- Startup incubation
- Enterprise and trade innovation

Together these locations form a distributed innovation network advancing Web3 development and real-world blockchain solutions across **Zambia and the broader Southern African region.

## Core Innovations

The hub supports a growing ecosystem of Cardano-based innovations including:

- **StartShield-STSH** – decentralized insurance and real-world asset infrastructure
- **UhuruLang** – African-first programming language for inclusive Web3 development
- **ASME-X Mining** – mining intelligence and mineral traceability platform
- **VillageChain Bank** – decentralized community banking infrastructure
- **STSH-Trade** – digital enterprise identity and AfCFTA trade facilitation platform
